Live music history

Maintain your own attendance catalogue and compare concerts and festivals with your listening history. Setlist.fm data is temporary and all gigs remain unverified.

Festival rule: three or more attended performances at the same named venue, on the same day or consecutive days, are automatically marked as an inferred festival. An explicitly supplied festival name always takes precedence.

Import gigs
