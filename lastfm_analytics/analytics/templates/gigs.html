Live music history

Maintain your own attendance catalogue and compare concerts and festivals with your listening history. Setlist.fm data is temporary and all gigs remain unverified.

Setlist.fm attendance synchronisation

Listening automatically imports performances you marked I Was There on setlist.fm. Enter your setlist.fm username and API key in the Home Assistant add-on settings. No CSV upload is required.

Checking import progress…

Import and subsequent checks are automatic, use a shared daily request budget and continue across restarts. Newly added attendances are checked at least weekly after an initial full import.

Gig catalogue

setlist.fm does not expose the festival name in its API. Listening automatically identifies festivals when three or more attended acts share a venue on one day or consecutive days. You can override any classification.
