Setlist.fm attendance synchronisation

Listening automatically imports performances you marked I Was There on setlist.fm. Enter your setlist.fm username and API key in the Home Assistant add-on settings. No CSV upload is required.

Checking import progress…

Import and subsequent checks are automatic, honour setlist.fm rate limiting and continue across restarts. Newly added attendances are checked at least weekly after an initial full import.
