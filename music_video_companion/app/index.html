Compatibility notes

The add-on seeks once at launch and once after Spotify resumes from a pause, but does not correct drift during a continuously playing song. Initial alignment requires the Chromecast to expose a seekable YouTube session. The app mutes the selected Chromecast, which may mute the television's shared audio output. Do not use it when you need TV sound.

Spotify and YouTube prohibit certain cross-service synchronisation and audio/video separation uses. This experimental add-on does not extract or modify any YouTube media.

If the YouTube receiver cannot be launched on your Chromecast, the error appears below and in Home Assistant logs.
