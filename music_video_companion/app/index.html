Compatibility notes

Playback is not frame-synchronised. The app mutes the selected Chromecast, which may mute the television's shared audio output. Do not use it when you need TV sound.

Spotify and YouTube prohibit certain cross-service synchronisation and audio/video separation uses. This experimental add-on does not extract or modify any YouTube media.

If the YouTube receiver cannot be launched on your Chromecast, the error appears below and in Home Assistant logs.
