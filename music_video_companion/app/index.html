Compatibility notes

The add-on seeks once at launch and once after Spotify resumes from a pause, but does not correct drift during a continuously playing song. Initial alignment requires the Chromecast to expose a seekable YouTube session. Manual audio control leaves your TV and soundbar volume untouched. Automatic audio control mutes a selected audio device when supported.

Spotify and YouTube prohibit certain cross-service synchronisation and audio/video separation uses. This experimental add-on does not extract or modify any YouTube media.

If the YouTube receiver cannot be launched on your Chromecast, the error appears below and in Home Assistant logs.
